ELI is the basis for the legal source model; PROV-O underpins provenance; SKOS structures the controlled vocabularies; the W3C n-ary relation pattern governs the trigger-event design; and OWL 2 provides the logical expressivity. Standard Deontic Logic, Hohfeld, Searle, Pollock, Bobbio, Kelsen, Nute, Alchourrón and Bulygin, and Dung ground the layers added in version 2.0, and the AI Act provides the worked examples.
