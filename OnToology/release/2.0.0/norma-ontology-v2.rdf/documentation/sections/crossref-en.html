Exception Defeaterc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/def/norma-o#ExceptionDefeater

A defeater that switches off the applicability of the defeated norm under a condition, without asserting the opposite deontic status.
Undercutting defeat in Pollock's sense. In SDL terms, undercutting O(X) yields not-O(X) — a weak permission to omit X — and not F(X). In Hohfeld's terms the Duty conferred by the defeated norm is withdrawn together with its correlative Claim, leaving the agent at liberty. NORMA records that outcome as legal positions (norma:Privilege with its norma:NoRight), never by minting a new norma:Permission individual, which would assert an explicit legislative grant that the source text does not make.
