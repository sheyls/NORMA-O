NORMA-O Derivation module: Overview back to ToC

The module describes a derivation run, the orderings it computed, the evaluation of each norm and the cycles found, together with three controlled vocabularies. The diagram below shows its classes and properties and their links to NORMA-O. Click the image to open the full-resolution version.

Conceptual groups

Derivation activity

normad:DerivationActivity (a prov:Activity) is one run of a rule set. It records the rule set and version (normad:ruleSetVersion), the named graph it wrote (normad:derivedGraph), its inputs (prov:used) and at least one assumption (normad:computedUnderAssumption).

Orderings and evaluations

normad:PrevalenceOrdering is the ladder obtained by ordering the norms along the prevalence graph, either static or for one case (normad:forFactPattern), with normad:stratumCount and normad:isAcyclic. Each normad:NormEvaluation belongs to one ordering (normad:inOrdering), concerns one norm (normad:evaluatedNorm) and carries its normad:prevalenceLevel, its normad:hasApplicabilityStatus and the parties it reaches (normad:reachesParty).

Cycles

normad:PrevalenceCycle is a cycle found in an ordering (normad:inCycle), involving at least two norms (normad:involvesNorm), classified by normad:hasCycleKind and listing the principles that compete on it (normad:citesCompetingPrinciple).

Controlled vocabularies

normad:ApplicabilityStatus (Invalid, NotActivated, Applicable, Defeated, Undecided), normad:Assumption (eight readings) and normad:CycleKind (SecondDegreeConflict, AnnotationContradiction, IrresolubleAntinomy), each a SKOS concept class with its concept scheme.
